01 Create your vault and a goal

Registers a device passkey (a prompt from your browser or platform, not this site) and creates one savings goal on-chain. This demo registers a single device; the vault's partner-device slot is left unusable.

02 Fund your wallet

Moves funds from your connected wallet into your family wallet's holding account. A standard wallet signature, no passkey needed for this step.
