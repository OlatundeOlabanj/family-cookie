How the passkey step actually works

Your connected wallet creates the goal and moves funds into a holding account. Releasing funds out of a goal is a separate step: your device (fingerprint, face, or PIN) signs that specific release with a secp256r1 passkey. The wallet alone cannot move goal funds on its own; the program checks a live device signature before anything moves.
